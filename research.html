Current Research Projects

Explore current research initiatives led by AIM Lab at NMSU in collaboration with NASA JPL and leading academic institutions.

🌌 Deep-Space Mission Design & Trajectory Optimization

Reinforcement Learning for Tour Design

NASA JPL

Developing tour design algorithms and contingency planning frameworks with trajectory reuse for complex multi-body space missions.

Collaboration: NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL)

Low-Thrust Trajectory Design Using a Shape-Based Approach

Establishing shape-based trajectory formulation incorporating necessary conditions of optimality for low-thrust propulsion systems.

Topic: Trajectory Optimization & Optimality Conditions

🤖 AI & Physics-Informed Machine Learning for Space Mechanics

Machine Learning Applications to Lambert’s Problem

NASA JPL

Constructing data-driven deep learning frameworks for fast, ultra-reliable prediction of Lambert’s orbital boundary-value problems.

Collaboration: NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL)

Machine Learning–Based Prediction of Optimal Deep Space Maneuvers

Applying machine learning models to predict the exact optimal location and timing of deep space trajectory maneuvers.

Topic: Deep Space Maneuvers & Neural Approximations

Physics-Informed Neural Network (PINN) Application to CRTBP

Investigating physics-informed neural network architectures to resolve two-point boundary-value problems in the Circular Restricted Three-Body Problem.

Topic: PINNs & Three-Body Orbital Dynamics

Low-Thrust Optimization Using Extreme Theory of Functional Connections (XTFC)

Leveraging the extreme theory of functional connections (XTFC) for rapid, efficient solutions of low-thrust optimal control problems.

Topic: XTFC & Optimal Control Theory

🛰️ Autonomous Deep-Space Navigation & Orbit Determination

Geometric Approach to Optical Navigation for Deep Space Missions

Iowa State

Integrating unified position/attitude measurement models into state estimation without requiring prior attitude or distance information.

Collaboration: Iowa State University

🪐 Multi-Body Dynamics & Libration Point Mechanics

Contour-Integral Methods in the Circular Restricted Three-Body Problem

Computing families of periodic orbits and analyzing zero-velocity region openings in the CRTBP via advanced contour-integral techniques.

Topic: Contour-Integral Methods & CRTBP Orbit Families
