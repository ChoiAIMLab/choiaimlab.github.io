Biography & Research Overview

Dr. Sungmoon Choi is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering at New Mexico State University (NMSU, 2026.08–Present) and the Director of the AeroAstro Intelligent Mechanics Lab (AIM Lab). He earned his Ph.D. in Aerospace Engineering with a minor in Computer Science from Iowa State University as a recipient of the Korean Government Scholarship Program for Study Overseas. During his doctoral studies, he served as a research intern at the NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) from June 2023 to December 2025. He also holds an M.S. in Aerospace Engineering from Iowa State University, an M.S. in Astronomy from Yonsei University in South Korea, and a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from Pusan National University in South Korea. His research lies at the intersection of astrodynamics, space mission design, trajectory optimization, orbit determination and space navigation, spacecraft Guidance, Navigation, and Control (GNC), and AI/ML applications.

  • Andrew Yi (NSF LAUNCH-SPACE Research Fellow) — University at Buffalo
    2026.06 – 2026.08

    Andrew earned his B.S. in Aerospace Engineering from the University at Buffalo. His research focuses on robust deep-space navigation using four-body angles-only optical analysis, aiming to resolve sign ambiguities and improve estimation accuracy through redundant celestial body observations.
