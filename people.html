Biography & Research Overview

Dr. Sungmoon Choi is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering at New Mexico State University (NMSU) and the director of the AeroAstro Intelligent Mechanics Lab (AIM Lab). His research lies at the intersection of astrodynamics, space mission trajectory optimization, optical navigation, and physics-informed machine learning. He is dedicated to advancing autonomous deep-space navigation and intelligent orbital mechanics.
