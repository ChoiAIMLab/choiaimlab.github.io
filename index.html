Core Research Thrusts

Pioneering Frontiers in Aerospace Mechanics

Astrodynamics & Mission Design

Trajectory optimization, multiple gravity-assist tour design, Mars aerobraking, and low-thrust trajectory formulation using dynamic-size genetic algorithms and shape-based approaches.

AI & Machine Learning for Space

Physics-informed neural networks (PINNs), reinforcement learning for mission tour design, and deep learning acceleration for Lambert’s problem and deep-space maneuvers.

Optical Navigation & Dynamic Systems

Angles-only deep-space initial orbit determination, multi-body orbital dynamics (CRTBP), and autonomous state estimation for next-generation planetary and deep-space missions.
