About AIM Lab

AIM Lab investigates fundamental and applied problems in orbital mechanics, space mission trajectory optimization, and autonomous deep-space navigation.

Research Thrusts

Astrodynamics & Mission Design

Multi-gravity assist tour design, trajectory optimization, low-thrust shape-based formulations.

AI & Physics-Informed ML

PINNs for CRTBP, neural network-based Lambert's problem solvers, reinforcement learning.

Optical Navigation

Angles-only deep-space initial orbit determination and geometric state estimation.
