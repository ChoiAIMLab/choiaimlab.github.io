Welcome to AIM Lab

The AeroAstro Intelligent Mechanics Lab (AIM Lab) at New Mexico State University specializes in astrodynamics, space mission design, deep-space optical navigation, spacecraft GNC, and physics-informed machine learning.

Lab Overview

The AeroAstro Intelligent Mechanics Lab (AIM Lab) at New Mexico State University (NMSU) is led by Prof. Sungmoon Choi. Our laboratory focuses on pioneering theoretical, computational, and algorithmic frameworks for next-generation space exploration and autonomous aerospace systems.

By combining celestial mechanics with cutting-edge artificial intelligence, machine learning, and optimization techniques, AIM Lab addresses challenging open problems in deep-space flight mechanics, trajectory design, space navigation, and multidisciplinary space system engineering.

The research interests of AIM Lab are not limited to astrodynamics or orbital mechanics, but span overall aerospace systems and machine learning. In addition, our work encompasses not only algorithmic or simulation development, but also fundamental mathematical foundations, theory, and beyond.

Core Research Interests

Explore Projects →
  • Astrodynamics & Celestial Mechanics: Multi-body orbital dynamics and chaotic motion.
  • Space Mission Design & Trajectory Optimization: Interplanetary trajectory design, gravity-assist sequencing, moon tour problems, and aerobraking.
  • Orbit Determination & Deep-Space Navigation: Angles-only optical navigation, initial orbit determination, state estimation, and autonomous spacecraft navigation.
  • Spacecraft Guidance, Navigation, and Control (GNC): Real-time trajectory generation, robust feedback control, and autonomous maneuver planning.
  • Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning: Reinforcement Learning, Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs), Extreme Theory of Functional Connections (XTFC), Diffusion Models, and deep learning approximations for orbital mechanics problems.

🚀 Prospective Students & Visiting Scholars

AIM Lab is actively looking for motivated Ph.D., M.S., and Undergraduate research students who are passionate about astrodynamics, space mission design, space navigation, and AI/ML applications in aerospace engineering.

Strong candidates typically possess a solid background in applied mathematics, astrodynamics, and machine learning, or related engineering disciplines, along with proficiency in programming (e.g., Python, MATLAB, Julia).

How to Apply for Prospective Students:

If you are interested in joining AIM Lab, please send an email to sungmoon.at.nmsu.edu with the subject line "[Prospective Student] Your Name - Program (Ph.D./M.S./Undergrad)" including:

  • Curriculum Vitae (CV)
  • Unofficial Academic Transcripts
  • A brief statement describing your research interests and background

🤝 Visiting Scholars & Postdoctoral Researchers:

AIM Lab warmly welcomes visiting scholars, sabbatical faculty, visiting Ph.D. students, and postdoctoral researchers who wish to conduct collaborative research in astrodynamics, space mission design, space navigation, and aerospace AI/ML applications. If you are interested in visiting or joint research collaborations, please contact Prof. Sungmoon Choi (sungmoon.at.nmsu.edu) with your CV.
